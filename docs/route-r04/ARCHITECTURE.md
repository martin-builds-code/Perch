# R04 initial design and comparison baseline

Verified baseline c230231d262b345418680c900a397acf76c826a0, R03 deployed app f2c1b3b3f0d4ede916f57994fccae3551717fc01; stable alias still R03 at investigation start. R01–R03 reports reviewed. Frozen R03 scheduler lives under tests/baseline. src/selectionR03.ts is a frozen selector used only in an explicitly authorised diagnostic trial, with a test re-export under tests/baseline. Neither shadow algorithm initiates provider requests.

No retained archive has two-dimensional bearing history. R01 observations are pseudonymous diagnostic summaries; R02 cases are one-off route records without aircraft positions; R03 is a reconstructed route acceptance replay. They cannot honestly be replayed as full CPA trajectories. Synthetic trajectories will be compared first; a new bounded live observation will preserve necessary derived, pseudonymised geometry for offline replay. It will not duplicate real provider traffic to run R03 concurrently.

The observer supplied elevation is now known. A server-only observation-elevation variable is used with existing private coordinates; neither is returned. Geometric altitude is preferred for estimated height above observer, with barometric fallback and explicit uncertainty about vertical datum/pressure effects. No terrain or microphone model. Legacy fixtures lacking relative height use an explicitly identified altitude proxy; an updated server reporting unknown height remains unknown.

One geographical provider response supplies all data. The server computes initial great-circle bearing and height relative to the observation point. Browser fields are derived bearing/height/reference only, never observation or aircraft coordinates. At <=15km the local east/north tangent approximation is adequate for short predictions. r=(d sin bearing, d cos bearing), v=groundspeed*(sin track, cos track), tCPA=-(r·v)/|v|². Predictions assume constant ground track/speed and altitude; no turn/climb prediction. Only fresh, valid positions and plausible 15–650kt motion qualify. True CPA is available only when it falls in the next 180s; horizon-boundary distance is never mislabeled CPA. Two consistent observations support prediction; initial vectors are provisional. Missing data falls back without invented trajectories.

Starting display contributions (not fitted detection probabilities): horizontal proximity32, elevation sine24, spatial proximity8, motion20, quality10, imminence6. Distance scales are 3km horizontally and 5km spatially; smooth terms avoid geographical eligibility cliffs. Close low aircraft retain substantial proximity; distant low aircraft gain little elevation relevance. Missing height uses a neutral half elevation/spatial contribution. The inherited factor name `altitude` now means elevation contribution, not low-altitude preference. Freshness penalty remains unchanged. Existing history bounds, 36s ordinary hold, 8pt hysteresis and 2 confirmations remain. Large handovers require two fresh confirmations; departing handovers permit two confirmations or established three-sample movement trends. Supported fast passes can take over before ordinary hold when within60s and meaningfully ahead. An exceptionally clear geometric mismatch (fresh challenger elevation>=60 degrees, distance<=1km, margin>=30; incumbent elevation<=15, distance>=8km) permits immediate correction, unless a position discontinuity was detected. Its warning now stays on the sample across cached reevaluations; repeated input cannot erase the warning. This trades single-sample caution for timely attention to an already overhead arrival. Failed feeds cannot confirm a challenger.

The 35-degree observation cone is a named diagnostic/prediction reference, not an eligibility cutoff. Cone entry/exit are distinct from horizontal CPA; both assume constant altitude as well as constant ground movement. A high jet can enter the useful sky before horizontal closest approach. Beyond180s CPA is unknown, even if the horizon endpoint suggests approach. No acoustic/visibility certainty or commercial-category preference enters display selection.

Enrichment uses predicted opportunity urgency, not the display score. At most six current future candidates are retained for planning; no bulk lookup. Required primary identity and urgent/passing/overhead primary routes remain protected. A supported fast secondary with close CPA or predicted cone entry can beat a less time-sensitive primary route when its urgency advantage exceeds15. Other eligible secondary routes precede optional primary metadata when live observation metadata exists. Unknown trajectories use conservative displayed-aircraft ordering. Genuine negative routes and positive caches keep their original TTLs and flight identity keys.

Low-horizon, distant secondary flights with no useful predicted approach wait for90s residency before spending a secondary slot. A useful approach releases the deferral; this applies to all categories and does not exclude helicopters or general aviation. Type/callsign information-value hints are deliberately small (commercial jet1.10, regional1.05, unknown1.0, GA0.90, helicopter0.85, registration-like callsign factor0.90 with total floor0.85). They are uncalibrated tie/near-tie heuristics, never airport inference or evidence of provider success. A close relevant helicopter can win both selection and a useful lookup. Native-identified, registration-known flights with a cached route miss or a supported fast peripheral/departing pass may skip optional operator lookup, preserving opportunities for routes. This may delay optional branding and is an explicit route-readiness tradeoff.

Any non-primary request, including one beyond the three displayed secondary cards, remains a secondary attempt under the collective90s gate. The global30s monotonic gate, single request, R01 local-deferral/upstream handling and caches remain binding. No new request timer. A90s budget can inherently be too slow for two jets arriving close together; R04 does not evade it or promise every fast pass a route.

Trial evidence is page-memory only:240 frames,32 pseudonymous aircraft per frame,1024 events,1024 lightweight evaluation times,1024 ID/flight mappings. Rows contain derived bearing/height, not aircraft or observer coordinates; callsigns become trial-local synthetic flight references and registrations are omitted. Diagnostic records stop at the verified server lease and are cleared on unmount. A dedicated `?mode=live&sky=1` view compares R03/R04 on identical snapshots; `?mode=live&view=normal` preserves the normal display and visible experimental warning. UI refreshes do not request providers. The optional server header `X-Aero-Position-Source` is emitted only inside the verified trial and distinguishes a single positional provider load from a cache/coalesced/blocked API response. Process-local gates are not a globally coordinated limiter.

Route urgency weights are named in OBSERVATION_POLICY:35 proximity (3km scale),30 predicted CPA elevation sine,20 immediacy (90s scale; earlier of cone entry or CPA),15 speed (saturated at250kt). Only supported fresh approaches expose non-zero scheduling urgency; initial estimates are half strength for diagnostics/display context. These are transparent trial heuristics, not fitted detection or HexDB-success probabilities.

The special stale-primary/fresh-challenger handover now requires a15s age advantage. A candidate merely120ms younger at the30s delayed boundary is not meaningfully fresher. This is a selection-stability guard, not a change to delayed/expired thresholds or the freshness score. Ordinary confirmed and fast/overhead takeovers remain available; expiry still releases immediately. Diagnostic evaluation times record every existing selector callback without adding a callback or provider request, enabling later replays to include challenger resets caused by ageing cached positions.
